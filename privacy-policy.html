Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026

This explains what Eltwo Expense collects when you scan or forward receipts, track spending, bills and gift cards, and manage subscriptions — and what we do (and don't do) with it.

Information we collect

Account information. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive the email address and a stable account identifier that provider gives us. If you tap "Try without an account," we create an anonymous identifier with no personal information attached — you can link an Apple or Google account to it later without losing your data.

Receipts & transactions. Photos of receipts you scan are uploaded to our servers and sent to Google's Gemini AI to read the merchant, date, totals, and line items. The receipt image and the resulting transaction record are stored in your account so you can review, edit, or delete them later.

Forwarded emails. If you use email receipts, each book gets its own forwarding address. Emails sent to it are received by our email provider and passed to our servers, where the sender is checked against the email addresses you have verified, and the email and its attachments are sent to Google's Gemini AI to find receipts, order confirmations and bills. When an email becomes a transaction, a copy of the receipt (the PDF, image or email body) is stored with it so you can view the original. Emails that aren't saved as a transaction wait in the app's email inbox and are deleted after 30 days; the activity log of received emails is also kept for 30 days. To confirm Gmail forwarding, we read the confirmation email Gmail sends to that address and show you its link.

Verified email addresses. If you add other email addresses you forward from, we store them and send a one-time code to confirm each one. Codes are deleted within a day.

Gift cards. Gift cards you add — the store, card number, PIN or barcode, balance, and any photos or eGift PDFs you attach — are stored in your account. Card numbers, PINs, barcodes and attached files are encrypted on our servers. On your device, attached files are kept encrypted and open only after Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode. Gift card photos and files are not sent to Gemini.

Recurring bills & budgets. Bills, budgets, books and other details you enter are stored in your account so they sync across your devices.

Subscriptions & purchases. Purchases go through Apple's In-App Purchase system. We receive a signed transaction record and an anonymous purchase token tied to your account — never your card number or billing details, which Apple handles directly.

Household & Family Group membership. If you create or join a shared Household, or a Family subscription group, we store which accounts belong to it so the app can enforce shared budgets or pooled scan quotas correctly.

How we use it

Turning a receipt photo or forwarded email into an itemized transaction, matching receipts to your bills and gift cards, keeping everything synced across your devices, calculating budgets, sales tax and spending reports, enforcing monthly scan quotas and applying purchased entitlements, sending you account emails (a welcome email and email-receipt setup instructions), and responding to support requests you send us. We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Sharing with other people

Two features share data with people you choose to connect with, and they work differently. A Household (shared ledger): members you invite can see the transactions logged in that household — this is the feature's whole purpose, a shared spending log. A Family Group (subscription): members share a pooled monthly scan quota only — your individual receipts and transactions are not visible to other Family Group members.

Gift cards are private to you unless you turn on sharing for a card; a shared card, including its number and attached files, is visible to the members of that book. Exports (such as CSV files) are prepared from your account, saved to your device, and go only where you send them.

Third-party services

Apple — Sign in with Apple, and all subscription/purchase billing via the App Store. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Google — Sign in with Google, and Gemini AI for reading receipt images and forwarded emails. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Cloudflare — receives emails sent to your forwarding address and passes them to our servers. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

Resend — delivers the emails we send you (welcome, setup instructions and verification codes). See Resend's Privacy Policy.

Storage & security

Data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections and stored on our servers. Gift card numbers, PINs, barcodes and attached files are additionally encrypted at rest. Database backups are kept for 30 days. Access to production data is limited to what's needed to operate and support the app.

Retention & your choices

You can delete individual transactions, receipts, emails, bills and gift cards at any time from within the app. To delete your entire account and its data, go to Settings → Account → Delete Account in the app, or email us at hello@eltwo.net and we'll process it promptly. Data in books shared with other members stays available to them. Deleted data is removed from backups within 30 days.

Children's Privacy

Eltwo Expense is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes materially, we'll update the effective date above. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the update.
