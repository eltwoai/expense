Privacy Policy

Effective September 7, 2026

This explains what Eltwo Expense collects when you scan receipts, track spending, and manage subscriptions — and what we do (and don't do) with it.

Information we collect

Account information. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive the email address and a stable account identifier that provider gives us. If you tap "Try without an account," we create an anonymous identifier with no personal information attached — you can link an Apple or Google account to it later without losing your data.

Receipts & transactions. Photos of receipts you scan are uploaded to our servers and sent to Google's Gemini AI to read the merchant, date, totals, and line items. The receipt image and the resulting transaction record are stored in your account so you can review, edit, or delete them later.

Subscriptions & purchases. Purchases go through Apple's In-App Purchase system. We receive a signed transaction record and an anonymous purchase token tied to your account — never your card number or billing details, which Apple handles directly.

Household & Family Group membership. If you create or join a shared Household, or a Family subscription group, we store which accounts belong to it so the app can enforce shared budgets or pooled scan quotas correctly.

How we use it

Turning a receipt photo into an itemized transaction, keeping it synced across your devices, calculating budgets and spending reports, enforcing monthly scan quotas and applying purchased entitlements, and responding to support requests you send us. We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Sharing with other people

Two features share data with people you choose to connect with, and they work differently. A Household (shared ledger): members you invite can see the transactions logged in that household — this is the feature's whole purpose, a shared spending log. A Family Group (subscription): members share a pooled monthly scan quota only — your individual receipts and transactions are not visible to other Family Group members.

Third-party services

Apple — Sign in with Apple, and all subscription/purchase billing via the App Store. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Google — Sign in with Google, and Gemini AI for reading receipt images. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Storage & security

Data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections and stored on our servers. Access to production data is limited to what's needed to operate and support the app.

Retention & your choices

You can delete individual transactions or receipts at any time from within the app. To request deletion of your entire account and associated data, email us at hello@eltwo.net and we'll process it promptly.

Children's Privacy

Eltwo Expense is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes materially, we'll update the effective date above. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the update.
