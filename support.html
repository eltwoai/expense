Does Eltwo Expense require an account?

No — tap "Try without an account" to use the app right away. You can link an Apple or Google account later without losing any data.

How do subscriptions and scan quotas work?

Each plan includes a monthly receipt-scan quota. Free, Plus, and Pro are individual plans; Family shares one pooled quota across up to 5 people. You can also buy a one-off top-up pack if you run out before your next renewal.

How do I delete my data?

Delete individual transactions or receipts any time from within the app. To delete your whole account, email hello@eltwo.net and we'll process it promptly.
